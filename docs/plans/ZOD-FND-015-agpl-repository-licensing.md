## Review Focus

- **Transitive and tool-only dependencies:** `pubspec.lock` holds roughly 100 packages (Melos and its tooling, test, analyzer, SDK packages). The inventory must separate what is in the shipped runtime closure from dev/test/tool-only packages, and must not omit the latter; whether tool-only packages fall under the Corresponding Source is an **Open** question, not a silent omission.
- **Binary content the pubspec does not show:** the Flutter engine embeds third-party libraries; Android/iOS builds add platform libraries (plan time: no member declares a native plugin). These cannot be verified from the lock file; they are listed and marked `Unverified` with the place to verify them (ZOD-ABT-007 / ZOD-REL-002 when a build exists).
- **Licenses with their own conditions:** MIT/BSD require the notice to travel with copies; OFL has its own conditions (no selling the font alone, Reserved Font Names); each is recorded with its condition. AGPL is not applied to third-party code; the `LICENSE` scope statement says so.
- **Provenance of project-owned assets:** a license can only be granted by whoever holds the rights. The owner states they created the logo, icons, design materials, specifications and translations (recorded as an owner statement, not independently verified), and both Git identities are theirs; third-party items inside those groups (font copies and similar) are carved out. AI-assisted authorship (the commit trailers name an AI co-author) is an **Open** legal question; the owner directed that it does not block implementation.
- **Source availability after the first build leaves the machine:** a public repository, tags that are later deleted or moved, a renamed or private repo, builds without a tag, and build inputs not in the repo (signing keys, SDK versions) would break "this build matches this tag". The policy covers each.
- **Swiss Ephemeris not yet vendored:** the inventory and notices carry a reserved, clearly labelled row for it (and its data files, Q2 open), so the later vendoring task has an exact place to fill and nothing implies it is already covered.
